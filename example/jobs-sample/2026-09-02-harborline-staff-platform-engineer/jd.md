## About the role

Harborline Robotics builds autonomous material-handling systems for warehouses.
Our fleet software runs across 400+ customer sites and every robot depends on
the platform this team owns.

We're looking for a Staff Software Engineer to own the infrastructure our fleet
services run on. You'll set technical direction for a team of 8, work across
three product groups, and be the person who decides how we run things in
production for the next five years.

## What you'll do

- Own the Kubernetes platform that all fleet services deploy onto
- Set the standard for observability across the engineering org — we have three
  competing tracing stacks and need one
- Design for multi-tenant isolation as we move from per-customer deployments to
  shared regional clusters
- Define SLOs with service owners and make on-call sustainable
- Build the Terraform and CI/CD paths that let a team ship a new service in days
- Mentor senior engineers and raise the technical bar through design review

## What we're looking for

- 8+ years building and operating distributed systems in production
- Deep Kubernetes experience — not just using it, but operating it
- Strong Go. Our platform services are Go; our fleet control plane is Rust
- Experience with OpenTelemetry or equivalent distributed tracing at scale
- Terraform and infrastructure as code
- You've designed multi-tenant systems and understand the isolation tradeoffs
- Track record defining SLOs and error budgets that teams actually adopt
- Experience mentoring senior engineers

## Nice to have

- Rust — the control plane is Rust and you'd be reviewing it
- Robotics, real-time systems, or hardware-in-the-loop testing
- Bazel — we're migrating the monorepo
- gRPC and protobuf at scale
- Experience in a regulated or safety-critical environment
