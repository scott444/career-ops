**Want:** Staff Software Engineer, Principal Engineer, Staff Platform Engineer,
Senior Staff Engineer — platform, infrastructure, developer experience
**Would accept:** Senior Software Engineer at a company where the scope is
genuinely staff-level and the title follows within a year (ask directly in the
screen; do not assume)
**No:** Engineering Manager, Director, or any role whose primary output is
headcount planning. Alex wants the IC track.

## Domain
**Want:** Developer platforms, infrastructure, data platforms, B2B SaaS with real
scale problems
**Open to:** Fintech, healthtech, logistics
**Avoid:** Adtech, gambling, defense primes, anything with "growth hacking" in
the team name

- Multi-team technical influence without direct reports
- A real on-call rotation (a team that does not carry a pager is a team whose
  reliability work is invisible and unrewarded)
- Engineers own their services end to end; no separate ops team that deploys

## Location
Portland, OR or remote US. Will do hybrid at 2 days/week if the office is in the
metro. No relocation.

## Comp floor
$245K base / $310K total. **Never state a number in outreach or in a first
screen.** If pushed, ask for their band first.

## Dealbreakers
- On-site 4+ days/week
- No equity or profit-sharing of any kind
- "We move fast and don't write tests" said approvingly
- A staff role with no examples of what the last staff engineer actually shipped
